## Compilation Note

This SOP was compiled on 2026-09-07 from session records maintained in
the CADMIES repository. This experiment note documents the Hieros
Origin Harvest as recorded in Session 008B.

## Experiment

**Date:** May 15, 2026
**Session:** Session 008B — Hieros Origin Harvest
**Input:** 7,900-line founding document containing the original
project vision
**Model:** Mistral 7B

## What Was Done

The original conversation containing Hieros' initial project vision —
a 7,900-line founding document — was fed to the harvest pipeline.
